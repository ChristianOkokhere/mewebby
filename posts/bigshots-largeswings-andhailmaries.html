Big Shots, Large Swings, and Hail Marys

Apr 25, 2026

Founders don't found like they used too, or at least it feels that way:

In Amazons first shareholder letter, Bezos told investors the company would "make bold rather than timid investment decisions.

That statement feels trivial now because Amazon is a winning buisnesses. It turns out that history has a way of making ambition seem obvious after the fact, but in 1997, this was not at all the obvious thing to say. At the time, Amazon was an online bookstore with losses, steep competition, brittle infrastructure, and a market that did not yet know what the internet would become. The safe, local decision would have been to build a better way to buy books, instead Bezos bet that commerce itself was about to be reorganized.

That distinction matters.

The great companies of the last technological cycle did not begin as polite software buisnesses. They began as attempts to own a future that did not exist yet. Amazon was not a bookstore. Tesla was not a car company. Airbnb was not a way to rent a room. Each of these comapnies were predicated on the belife that a market, or entire category of commerce could be remade.

The current crop of founders do not seem to found like that anymore, or at least the median founder imagination feels smaller.

This isn't due to a lack of talent. In many ways this is the most equiped that the average person has ever been to take on the word. People have better models, better tooling, better distribution, better templates, more examples to draw inspiration from. Companies, products, or ideas that once took significant manpower and time investent can now be prototyped, and validated within days. The cost of creation, and the access to inteligence have collapsed.

Instead of birthing this golden age of value creation, in the short term AI has seemingly placed a cloud over our ability to see beyond the present moment. For many, totaly relaxing the previous baries to value creating has created a stong facsimile for porgress, barring people from the outlier outcomes that make creating something new truly exciting.

When you can can solve any problem every workflow begins to look like a startup, every annoyance becomes a product, and every product has an obvious buyer. This is not nothing. Many of these endevaors will become useful. Some will be profitable. Some will sell quickly and make everyone involed look rational, but usefulness is not consequence. A company can work and still not matter. It takes a cetain______.

So how do you build a sexy company that people really care about?
